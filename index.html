Record one 10-second voice memo each day. Play any day back, or stitch every clip into a montage. It all stays on your iPhone.

The three screens

Mockups of the current iPhone app. The Days and Montage screens use a few example clips so the list isn’t empty — not recordings from anyone.

What it does

Today
One clip a day, up to ten seconds. Tap the red mic to record. If you want a different take, re-record today and the earlier clip is replaced.
Days
Every saved day shows up in a calendar and a list. Play any clip back, on the phone.
Montage
Build a single playback of every clip, in date order. Share or export that file if you choose.
On this iPhone
Recordings are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account. The app is meant to work offline. An optional daily reminder, if you turn it on, is a local notification at a time you choose.
