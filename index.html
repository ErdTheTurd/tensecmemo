Tensec Memo

Ten seconds a day. A year you can hear.

Record a 10-second voice memo for the day. Listen back to any day, or play your clips in order as a montage. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Coming soon to iPhone

How it works

  1. Record today’s memo

    One clip a day, up to ten seconds. If you want a different take, re-record today and the earlier clip is replaced.

  2. Listen back to any day

    Past days with a clip appear in a list or calendar. Play a moment whenever you like, on the phone, without an account.

  3. Hear the year as a montage

    Clips are stitched in date order so you can play them as one piece, and share or export that montage if you choose.

Private, on this device

Recordings are stored only on your iPhone, with complete file protection. The app is meant to work offline. There is no account to create, and the app does not include analytics. An optional daily reminder, if you turn it on, is a local notification at a time you choose.
